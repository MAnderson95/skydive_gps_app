What these mean
Altitude
Height above the ground at takeoff/landing, from the phone's barometer.
Vert Speed
Descent rate, from barometric altitude change. 3-second rolling average.
Gnd Speed
Horizontal speed over the ground, from GPS position change. 3-second rolling average.
Glide · FF / CP
Horizontal distance covered per foot of altitude dropped. FF = freefall, since exit; CP = canopy, since opening. A per-phase summary, not a live instant number.
Distance
Total horizontal ground track covered since exit.
